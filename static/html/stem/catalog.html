/stem/ sticky
Welcome to /stem/, a board dedicated to serious discussion of science and technical fields.

What belongs here:
>Mathematics (pure & applied)
<Physics, chemistry, biology
>Engineering disciplines
<Computer science, programming, AI
>Research papers, textbooks, problem-solving
<Academic and career advice in STEM fields

Guidelines
>Stay on topic. Off-topic threads may be removed.
<Effortposting is encouraged. Low-effort memes and shitposts will be deleted.
>When asking questions, include context and show your attempt.
<Cite sources when possible (papers, books, etc.).
>Be respectful—debate ideas, not people.

Help threads
>Allowed, but don’t just dump questions and ask for direct solution
>Show your work or explain what you don’t understand.
>No chamar cheating requests.

Prohibited
<Pseudoscience, misinformation, or conspiracy spam
<Academic dishonesty (paid solutions, impersonation, etc.)
<Reposts and spam

keep it sharp, informative, and worth reading.
