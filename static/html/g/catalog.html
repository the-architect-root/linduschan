Guidelines for /g/
Welcome to the main hub for all things tech. Use this thread for ongoing discussions that don’t need their own post.

What goes here:

>General tech questions and advice
>PC builds, upgrades, and troubleshooting
>Programming, software, and development talk
>Gadgets, hardware, and peripherals
>OS discussions (Windows, Linux, macOS, etc.)
>Industry news, trends, and hot takes

Basic guidelines:

>Search before asking repeated questions
>Be clear about your setup when asking for help
>No spam or low-effort posts
>Keep it civil and on-topic

Happy tech'ing
